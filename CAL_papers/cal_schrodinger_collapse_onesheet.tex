{\Large\bfseries One-sheet: Schr\"odinger from the bare CAL at $\alpha\to1^-$}\par
\textit{Schr\"odinger evolution from the bare Causal Accumulation Law at $\alpha\to1^-$: feedback on the accumulated past, unitarity in the limit, and carrier entanglement as time dilation.} S.~Leizerman, LIOR Research Initiative, November 13, 2026. Script: \texttt{cal\_edge\_transducer.py} (numpy only, about 15\,s).

\textbf{Inputs.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item The CAL as written: $T=\alpha J-(1-\alpha)\int k\,\Pi P\Phi\,J$.
\item The free, flat sector: $\Pi P\Phi=1$, an order-$\alpha$ power-law memory, and a spatial profile $\hat g(k)$.
\item The phase belongs to the source: order-$\alpha$ accumulation turns $e^{iS/\hbar}$ by $\alpha$ quarter turns.
\item The update rule $J_{n+1}=T_n$, a feedback loop on the accumulated past.
\item The current's past rotated one way.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Not used:} a phase prefactor, a coupling constant, a time unit, a double scaling, or $T=0$ as a law. Time is counted in radians of the carrier's own rotation.

\textbf{Changed from the earlier version.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item The prefactor, $r_1$, $\tau_c$, the double scaling and $T=0$ are removed.
\item Unitarity is now exact only at the edge, not for every $\alpha<1$.
\item The mass formula is $\hbar/2m=\langle r^2\rangle/12$ in rest radians.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Open.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
\item A rigorous remainder estimate for the asymptotics. It is formal now, and confirmed numerically to $1-\alpha=10^{-7}$.
\item The equivalence condition, a kernel width of $\sqrt6$ Compton wavelengths. It is a forward pointer only.
\item Interaction, which needs the return leg (order $2$).
\end{itemize}
